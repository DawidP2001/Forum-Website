1769 August 15: Napoleon is born in Corsica, a fiery child destined to shake Europe’s established order.
1785 October 28: Napoleon graduates from military academy, leaving behind a young cadet and stepping into the world with cold ambition.
1793 September 18: Napoleon captures Toulon from the British, using daring strategy to turn a vulnerable port into his first famous victory.
1796 March 2: Napoleon is appointed commander of the Army of Italy, and his thunderous leadership begins to rewrite the rules of warfare.
1797 October 17: Napoleon signs the Treaty of Campo Formio with Austria, shaping a new balance of power from the ruins of war.
1798 May 19: Napoleon invades Egypt, chasing glory on foreign sands while Europe watches the bold expedition unfold.
1799 November 9: Napoleon overthrows the Directory in a coup d'état, seizing control and carving a path from general to ruler.
1804 December 2: Napoleon crowns himself Emperor of the French, transforming revolutionary chaos into imperial grandeur.
1805 October 21: Napoleon defeats the Third Coalition at the Battle of Austerlitz, delivering one of the greatest tactical triumphs in military history.
1806 October 14: Napoleon defeats Prussia at the Battle of Jena-Auerstedt, crushing enemies with swift maneuver and iron resolve.
1807 June 14: Napoleon defeats Russia at the Battle of Friedland, forcing a reluctant peace after a campaign of relentless pressure.
1808 February 2: Napoleon invades Spain, igniting a brutal guerrilla war that will sap the strength of his empire.
1812 June 24: Napoleon invades Russia, embarking on a fateful campaign whose bitter winter will become legend.
1813 October 16-19: Napoleon is defeated at the Battle of Leipzig, a massive clash that marks the beginning of his downfall.
1814 April 11: Napoleon abdicates and is exiled to Elba, a fallen emperor sent to a rocky island with the remnants of his crown.
1815 March 20: Napoleon escapes from Elba and returns to France, sparking the dramatic Hundred Days and a final desperate resurgence.
1815 June 18: Napoleon is defeated at the Battle of Waterloo, ending his dream in a muddy field watched by the world.
1815 October 15: Napoleon abdicates again and is exiled to Saint Helena, removed to a lonely rock far from Europe’s bustle.
1821 May 5: Napoleon dies on Saint Helena, closing the chapter on a life of brilliance, ambition, and ruin.
